A shift on the IAM desk

Labs arrive the way work does: as tickets, emails and alerts. Nothing tells you which ones are traps. See three labs worked screen by screen.

  1. A ticket lands in your queue

    A charge nurse starts Monday, a radiographer transfers, a contractor's end date passed last week. Some requests are urgent. Some are not what they say they are.

  2. You check it against the source of truth

    PeopleHub says who works here, in which role, for whom. The access policy says what that role gets. "Copy her colleague's access" is not a justification under minimum necessary.

  3. You make the change

    In ADUC, PowerShell, the Entra admin center or the Okta console, whichever the job calls for. Disable before you remove. Revoke sessions when someone is walked out.

  4. The lab grades what actually happened

    Checks read the directory, the cloud tenants and the ticket, not your intentions. Fall for a trap and Copilot tells you which control just broke.

A mentor that watches the lab, not just the chat

IAM Copilot re-checks every running lab after each change. Disable the wrong nurse or add a service account to Domain Admins and it speaks up straight away, the way a senior colleague looking over your shoulder would.

Chat runs on your own computer through Ollama. It never decides whether you passed: the checks do.

Copilot You just added svc-backup to Domain Admins. Lab "Service account in Domain Admins" now fails a critical check: Tier 0 membership.

Why is that wrong? The backup job needs to read everything.

Copilot It needs Backup Operators on the servers it backs up, not control of the domain. A service account with a static password in Tier 0 is the easiest path to the whole forest. Remove it, grant the specific right, and note the change on the ticket.

Install in a couple of minutes

Your world, settings and progress are saved on your computer and kept through updates.

  1. Download the installer

    Meridian IAM Range Setup for Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

  2. Run it

    The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may stop it. Choose More info, then Run anyway. It installs for your account without administrator rights.

  3. Sign in as Admin

    The default password is shown on the sign-in screen: Meridian!2026. Open IAM Coach from the taskbar and start your first lab.

Automatic updates. The app checks for new releases when it starts and every six hours, downloads them in the background, and installs them when you restart.

Optional AI. Install Ollama and run ollama pull llama3.2 to turn on Copilot chat and AI-designed labs.

Rather not install? Open the web edition: the same lab in your browser. For Copilot chat there, allow only this site in Ollama (setx OLLAMA_ORIGINS "https://this-site", then restart Ollama), never "*".

Prefer the source? Clone the repository and run npm ci then npm run dev in range/.

Erick Omari

Identity and access management

I built Meridian IAM Range because learning IAM from slides does not prepare anyone for a charge nurse locked out at the start of a night shift, or a "copy her colleague's access" request that would hand a contractor patient records. Everything here favours realistic failure over scripted success: every task has a way to get it wrong, every mistake leaves a log line, and every log line is something you can talk through in an interview.

It is also how I practise: the labs, the traps and the grading are the work I want to be trusted with.
